This is the user's first quant project. They are learning the end-to-end quant research workflow (data → features → models → backtest → risk) as they go.

**How to apply:** When explaining quant concepts (walk-forward, leakage, Sharpe, drawdown, factor construction, etc.), give the intuition alongside the math. Don't assume prior familiarity with terms like "cross-sectional rank," "survivorship bias," or "look-ahead bias" — briefly ground them. Prefer interpretable baselines (linear regression, simple momentum) before jumping to complex models.
